Liberation Mono glyph coverage maps (rEFInd 0.14.2 fonts/liberation-mono-
regular-12/14/24/28.png, the images rEFInd embeds as its built-in font) are
compiled into CHARON.EFI and CHARONVOICE.EFI as src/efi/CharonAaFonts.h
(generated by tools/gen-charon-aafonts.py) to recognize text rEFInd draws.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (LICENSE.txt); the data is not a font
and does not use the Reserved Font Names.
