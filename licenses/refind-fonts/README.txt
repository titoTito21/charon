Glyph coverage maps of all fonts shipped with rEFInd 0.14.2 (fonts/*.png,
12-28 px) are compiled into CHARON.EFI and CHARONVOICE.EFI as
src/efi/CharonAaFonts.h (tools/gen-charon-aafonts.py) to recognize text that
rEFInd and its themes draw. The data is not a font.
