Charon r409 - every Polish and English RHVoice voice in the RHVoice add-on (2026-11-23)
=====================================================================================

Request: the external RHVoice synthesizer module (\EFI\HAK\synths\rhvoice.tts) must offer ALL English and
ALL Polish RHVoice voices, selectable in Charon; and (added during the work) every voice needs a stable
identifier that maps to the voice's name on the systems Charon installs.

Result in one paragraph: 13 voices (5 Polish, 8 English) are offered, all load and speak with the shipped
engine RHVoice 1.18.5 (no engine update needed, none blocked). Natan and Slt stay the defaults (voice
indices 0 and 1, byte-identical packs). Each further voice is one optional data file. Three voices are
non-commercial (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and three carry no licence file upstream - see section 2. Nothing was
released; the packagers' part is section 8.

Paths below are relative to the folder that holds work/ and outputs/.

2. LICENCES - what needs a decision
-----------------------------------
a) NON-COMMERCIAL: Cezary, Evgeniy-Eng, Lyubov are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The licence allows copying and sharing
   the unmodified material, in whole or in part and in any format, for non-commercial purposes with
   credit; it forbids commercial use and sharing adapted material. Charon is given away free and ships
   each voice's 16 kHz files byte-identical (lossless container only), so they are INCLUDED, with the
   notice in LICENCES.txt (capital-letter paragraph: who sells or commercially uses a copy must delete
   three named files), in RHVOICE-VOICES.txt and as "| non-commercial" on the voice-licence lines of
   rhvoice.manifest. This is a reading of the licence, not legal advice; the READMEs of Evgeniy-Eng and
   Lyubov say integration "into any product" can be requested from rhvoice@tiflo.org. If the user
   prefers not to carry them: package-synths-r409.py medium_files(non_commercial=False) leaves the three
   files out (the module then simply does not offer them).
b) NO LICENCE FILE: Alan, Bdl, Clb. Their repositories and official archives contain no licence text.
   Basis for including them: RHVoice's doc/en/License.md (shipped as licences/rhvoice-License.md) lists
   restrictions for some voices and none for these; Debian's copyright file (rhvoice 1.14.0-3) files
   them under data/* = GPL-2+. Earlier (r393) Alan was left out for exactly this reason, so this is a
   change of that decision - the user should confirm it. Removing them = deleting three .chsp files.
   Their origin in CMU ARCTIC (bdl, clb, awb) is NOT verified from an official source.
c) Other attributions (CC BY 4.0 Alicja, Magda; CC BY-SA 4.0 Dasha-Eng; CMU ARCTIC Ksp, Slt; CC0 Natan,
   Michal): names and licence texts are in LICENCES.txt and licences/ of the add-on folder.
Where it is visible: \EFI\HAK\synths\LICENCES.txt, RHVOICE-VOICES.txt, licences\ (25 files), the manifest.
Spoken licence notes are not implemented (designed separately); the manifest lines
"voice-licence = <id> | <licence>[ | non-commercial]" are there for that work (Charon's and
Charon-Settings.ps1's manifest parsers ignore unknown keys; checked in the source).
